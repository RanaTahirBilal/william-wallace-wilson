Founded in 2023. Four times the size today.

Wally Wilson opened Wilson Risk Partners in April 2023 with $950,000 in revenue. In 2024 he affiliated the agency with Heffernan, an insurance firm based in Walnut Creek, California, choosing that partnership over aggregators or equity groups. Career

From Joplin to Pittsburg

Born in Joplin, Missouri, Wally Wilson started at his father's insurance agency there in 1970, the year he graduated from the University of the South in Sewanee. He moved to Kansas City in 1986 and built a long career in excess and surplus lines and group program development before coming to Pittsburg in 2016 to lead Ryan Insurance.
